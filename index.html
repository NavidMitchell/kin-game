Move. Jump. Fire.

Left thumb · Movement pad

Slide left or right to move.
Slide down to crouch.

Up no longer jumps. Upward aiming is coming later.

Right thumb · Two buttons

Press Jump / Fly to jump. Hold for height or jetpack flight. Release before jumping again.

Hold Fire to keep shooting. Jumping never fires a shot.
